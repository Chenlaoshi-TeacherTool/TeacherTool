    "Fakebook" character profile

    Use this page to build an imaginary profile for a book character, historical figure, scientist, or event you are studying.

    1. Enter a character name, then add a cover photo and profile photo.
    2. Fill in the intro with facts, quotes, or details in character.
    3. Add friends — other people connected to your character — with names and photos.
    4. Write 1–2 posts as your character, then scroll to a post and reply in character as one of the friends.

    Your work saves automatically in this browser. Use Export backup if you want a copy to keep or move to another computer.
