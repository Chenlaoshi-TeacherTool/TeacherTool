How to use Sunflower Classroom Spinner in class

Add terms, questions, student numbers, topics, or challenge levels to a sunflower wheel and use the result to begin a classroom task.

Best for: K–12 Prep time: 3–5 minutes

What is this tool?

This is a customizable random spinner for any subject. A spin should lead to a clear action: define, solve, compare, sketch, justify, demonstrate, or ask a follow-up question.

How to use it

  1. Add 8–16 items from one lesson or review goal so the labels stay readable.
  2. Model exactly what students do after the wheel stops.
  3. Give quiet thinking time before asking for an answer, then invite a second student to add evidence or another method.

Classroom example: math representation review

Add “number line,” “equation,” “table,” “diagram,” and “words” to the wheel. After a spin, students represent the same problem in the selected form and explain what the representation makes easier to see.

Teacher tips

  • Use student numbers instead of full names when the wheel is projected.
  • Mark easier and stretch items so students can enter the task at different levels.
  • Allow students to request a hint or a partner after a random selection so participation stays safe.
